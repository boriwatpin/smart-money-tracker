How to read this: Click a card to see full top holdings. "Value Δ Q/Q" is the change in total 13F portfolio value quarter-over-quarter — a blended proxy for gain/loss (13F filings don't disclose entry price or realized P&L, so this mixes trading activity with market moves). A NEW badge means the fund didn't hold that CUSIP last quarter; a +X% badge means an existing position grew by 15% or more in share count. For both, we estimate a price as the average daily closing price during the quarter (SEC doesn't disclose exact trade dates) compared to today's price — a rough approximation, not a real cost basis. No estimate is shown for stable existing positions since there's no reliable way to infer their original cost basis at all. 13F filings are due 45 days after quarter-end: Feb 14, May 15, Aug 14, Nov 14.
