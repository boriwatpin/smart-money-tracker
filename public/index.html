How to read this: Click a card to see full top holdings. "Value Δ Q/Q" is the change in total 13F portfolio value quarter-over-quarter — a blended proxy for gain/loss (13F filings don't disclose entry price or realized P&L, so this mixes trading activity with market moves). A NEW badge means the fund didn't hold that CUSIP last quarter; a +X% badge means an existing position grew by 15% or more in share count. For both, we estimate a price as the average daily closing price during the quarter (SEC doesn't disclose exact trade dates) compared to today's price — a rough approximation, not a real cost basis. "Position history" tracks a holding's % of portfolio across stored quarters — a "—" means it wasn't in that fund's top 25 that quarter, not necessarily that they didn't hold it at all. "Notable moves" only shows moves worth more than 1% of a fund's book (and, where priced, more than a 1% price move) to cut out noise. 13F filings are due 45 days after quarter-end: Feb 14, May 15, Aug 14, Nov 14.
