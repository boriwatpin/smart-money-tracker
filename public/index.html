How to read this page
Data. Everything comes from 13F filings, which large funds file within 45 days of each quarter end (Feb 14, May 15, Aug 14, Nov 14). They show US-listed long positions at quarter end only: no shorts, no trade dates, no entry prices.
Fund cards. Click a card to see its top holdings. "Value Δ Q/Q" is the change in total 13F portfolio value, which mixes trading with market moves. New means the fund didn't hold that stock last quarter; +X% means the share count grew 15% or more. "Position history" tracks a holding's % of portfolio across quarters; "—" means it wasn't in the fund's top 25 that quarter, not necessarily that it wasn't held.
Winners & losers estimates each position's cost from the quarters it was bought in and compares it with the latest close. See "How this is calculated" in that section.
This quarter's activity groups last quarter's buying and selling by stock (Signals) and by fund (Moves). Moves only lists changes worth more than 1% of a fund's book, and where priced, more than a 1% price move.

Winners & losers
Estimated returns on each fund's top 25 positions, plus the trades they've exited.
How this is calculatedEstimated cost basis vs. the latest market close for each fund's top 25 positions, plus the trades they've exited. 13F filings never disclose trade prices, so cost is estimated: shares added in a quarter are priced at that quarter's average daily close (split-adjusted), sells keep the average cost. The cost history comes from a deeper top-100 archive, so positions that grew into the top 25 keep their earlier buys. Open returns assume the fund still holds the position, which may no longer be true given the 45-day filing lag.

This quarter's activity
What the funds did in their latest filings, grouped by stock or by fund. Describes past filings only; not a prediction or investment advice.
How signals are classifiedCriteria (checked in this order):
Battleground — funds are both buying and exiting this quarter, and neither side has 75%+ of the activity (a genuine split).
Distribution — funds are exiting with no buyers, or exiting represents 75%+ of a mixed quarter's activity.
Broad consensus — held by a majority of tracked funds with no meaningful selling (or buying represents 75%+ of a mixed quarter's activity and the majority threshold is met).
Building — one or more funds newly buying or adding, not yet at majority.
Watching — held steadily by 2+ funds, no notable activity this quarter.
