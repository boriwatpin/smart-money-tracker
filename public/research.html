📈 Broadening Consensus

Rule: stocks where the number of funds holding it has increased across the last 4 stored quarters (not flat or declining), sorted by size of the increase.

🌱 Fresh Conviction, Not Yet Priced In

Rule: positions flagged NEW or INCREASED this quarter, with an estimated price move of less than 5% (up or down) since the fund's average estimated cost — i.e. the market hasn't moved much yet since the disclosed buying happened.

✅ Broad Consensus + Price Confirmation

Rule: held by 2+ funds, where at least one fund's estimated price move since their average cost is positive — i.e. multiple funds agree, and the price has moved in the direction of that thesis so far.
