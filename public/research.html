Locked cohort performance

Once a quarter's filings are complete across all tracked funds, this locks in the top new positions (combined by CUSIP where multiple funds independently bought the same stock) and tracks their price from that lock date forward — a real, dollar-based track record, not just a point-in-time screen like the lists below.

Broadening consensus

Rule: stocks where the number of funds holding it has increased across the last 4 stored quarters (not flat or declining), sorted by size of the increase.

Fresh conviction, not yet priced in

Rule: positions flagged as new or increased this quarter, with an estimated price move of less than 5% (up or down) since the fund's average estimated cost — i.e. the market hasn't moved much yet since the disclosed buying happened.

Broad consensus with price confirmation

Rule: held by 2+ funds, where at least one fund's estimated price move since their average cost is positive — i.e. multiple funds agree, and the price has moved in the direction of that thesis so far.
